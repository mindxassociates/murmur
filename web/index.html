Models & Settings

Your voice

Record a short sample of your own voice. After a session is transcribed, speaker separation uses this sample to recognize which speaker is you, so the transcript can be labeled Therapist and Client automatically. The sample is stored only on this device, and each device keeps its own.

Read this aloud (about 30 seconds):

Hello, I am the therapist for this session. In our time together today, we will talk about what has been on your mind, how you have been sleeping, and anything that has felt difficult lately. There are no right or wrong answers here. We can go at whatever pace feels comfortable, and you can pause or stop me at any time if something does not feel clear.

Prefer not to read? Speaking naturally for 25–30 seconds works just as well.

Practice settings

Security & HIPAA safeguards

Murmur is built to support your HIPAA compliance program: client data is encrypted on this device (SQLCipher / AES-256), never transmitted anywhere, access requires a personal account, and sign-ins and changes are recorded in a tamper-evident audit log. The project folder's HIPAA.md maps each safeguard and lists the organizational steps (policies, risk analysis, workforce training) that remain yours.
